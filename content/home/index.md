Renderer-agnostic 3D scene and world presentation storage for Jess, a Luau package.

<!-- more -->

A [Jess](https://github.com/DreamWeave-MP/jess) package, `org.dreamweave.scene`. Add it to a `jess.toml` and
`jess workspace sync` pins it from this site:

```toml
[dependencies]
"org.dreamweave.scene" = "0.1"
```

Every release below is the package's own archive, with its content digest beside the archive's
SHA-256; `jess.lock` pins the first and checks both.
